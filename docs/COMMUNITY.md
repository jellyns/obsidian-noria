# Noria Community

[中文](#中文) · [README](../README.md)

Share how you use Noria, ask for help, and suggest improvements to workflows, appearance, interactions, or settings.

## Join the conversation

[Read the Noria introduction and join the discussion on Obsidian Forum](https://forum.obsidian.md/t/noria-connecting-notes-projects-tasks-and-reviews-in-obsidian/118107).

[Join Noria on Discord](https://discord.gg/JjCyDvFvgY) — English and Chinese are welcome.

Start with `start-here` for installation, guides, and community rules. Ask questions in `help-and-feedback`, share workflows in `showcase`, and follow `announcements` for updates.

## Get started

- [Install Noria](../README.md#installation)
- [Read the user guide](USER-GUIDE.md)
- [Report a problem](https://github.com/jellyns/obsidian-noria/issues/new?template=bug_report.md)
- [Suggest an improvement](https://github.com/jellyns/obsidian-noria/issues/new?template=feature_request.md)

## Feedback that helps

Describe what you were trying to do, what happened, and what you expected. For a bug, include your Noria and Obsidian versions, operating system, and steps to reproduce it. Screenshots are especially useful for layout and interaction feedback.

You can ask for help in the community without opening a GitHub issue. The maintainer can help turn a reproducible report into an issue so that its resolution remains easy to find.

Please follow the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Report security vulnerabilities through the [Security Policy](../SECURITY.md).

欢迎交流 Noria 的使用方法，分享工作流，并提出功能、界面美观、交互和设置方面的改进建议。

### 加入交流

[在 Obsidian Forum 阅读 Noria 介绍并参与讨论](https://forum.obsidian.md/t/noria-connecting-notes-projects-tasks-and-reviews-in-obsidian/118107)。

从 `start-here` 查看安装、手册和社区规则，在 `help-and-feedback` 求助与反馈，在 `showcase` 分享工作流，在 `announcements` 查看更新。

### 开始使用

- [安装 Noria](../README.zh-CN.md#安装)
- [阅读用户手册](zh-CN/USER-GUIDE.md)

### 怎样描述问题

说明你想完成什么、实际发生了什么，以及预期结果。反馈 Bug 时，请附上 Noria 和 Obsidian 版本、操作系统与复现步骤；反馈布局和交互问题时，截图会更有帮助。

你可以直接在社区求助，无需先创建 GitHub Issue。可复现的问题由维护者协助整理到 Issues，便于追踪处理结果。

交流遵循[行为准则](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)。安全漏洞请按[安全说明](../SECURITY.md)反馈。
